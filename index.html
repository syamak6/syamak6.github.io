About

I am a computer science academic and researcher specialising in machine learning, deep learning and bioinformatics. I teach undergraduate Computer Science, Big Data and AI at the University of Wollongong in Dubai, and I hold a PhD in Computer Science from BITS Pilani, Dubai Campus.

My research develops computational tools for metagenomic data. I use graph neural networks, transformers and large language models to predict disease from the human gut microbiome, identify microbial biomarkers, detect antibiotic resistance genes and predict microbe–drug associations. Alongside research, I have more than a decade of university teaching experience, and I enjoy mentoring students on research projects.

Research Interests

Disease prediction from the gut microbiome

Graph-based and deep learning models, such as boosted GraphSAGE and deep forests, that predict disease from human gut metagenomic profiles.

Antibiotic resistance genes

Cross-feature transformers and ensembles of fine-tuned large language models that identify resistance genes directly from metagenomic sequences.

Microbial biomarkers and drug associations

Microbial interaction networks, metapath node embeddings and self-supervised learning to find biomarkers and predict microbe–drug links.

Sequence modelling

Bidirectional LSTMs and other sequence models for gene prediction in metagenomic data.
